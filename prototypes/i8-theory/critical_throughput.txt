NOTE on the ceiling: the CRC leg is wire-invisible (byte-identical output).
The materialization and concat legs are NOT free -- lazy materialization
defers work, it does not delete it. The 'middle' scenario assumes half of
each is genuinely eliminable; that is an ASSUMPTION, stated as such.
Entropy-codec selection lives inside the token loop (11% of
decode), of which the entropy pulls themselves are 0.3% => the maximum
speedup obtainable by per-stream codec choice is ~1.003x. That is
why S6-1 measured zero decode effect from budget selection: it is not a
tuning failure, it is the size of the target.
