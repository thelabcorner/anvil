# Brotli implementation mining for ANVIL's Pareto objective
*Source-attested research, 2026-11-19. Research: VERIFY / REFINE; execution: no new codec run, no CI dispatch, no benchmark.*

## The user-directed objective remains invariant

ANVIL seeks strict multidimensional improvement against mainstream lossless codecs over **complete compressed bytes, encode elapsed time, decode elapsed time, peak RSS, charged decoder distribution assets, deterministic byte-exactness, streaming/access behavior and safety**, on independently preregistered reference operating points and disjoint heldouts. The Hutter Prize is an inspiration repository, not ANVIL's objective. **Brotli itself is a particularly useful design prior** because its encoder explicitly varies model depth, match-search cost, table memory and decoder cost with the desired operating point. Do not replace a measured six-axis goal with a Brotli-quality-11 compressed-byte contest.

## Important distinction: source-derived design lesson vs ANVIL experiment

Brotli's adaptive *encoder* decisions are not an encoding-independent mathematical theorem. Its historical thresholds (0.2 bits per symbol, 7.92 entropy, 13-byte sampling) are engineered settings, **not universal constants** and not a guarantee of any ANVIL compression ratio or Pareto gain. They would require the same fixed source/format controls and independent confirmation as a new mechanism. In particular, Brotli's decoder uses a fast table-based Huffman path; ANVIL's expensive BWT arithmetic path cannot simply “borrow” that throughput without a different entropy coder and likely a new wire mode.

ANVIL's own pinned q11 backend calls `BrotliEncoderCompress(11,30,BROTLI_MODE_GENERIC)` and must be fully charged when selected; the automatic BWT-vs-Brotli path **actually executes both arms** before selecting smaller output. Brotli `ShouldCompress` cannot by itself certify that skipping the other backend preserves the exact smallest wire bytes. The A91 independently preregistered stream-prefix/fallback oracle covers that harder question; do not duplicate it as a vague classifier.

## One tightly scoped next research decision after A70 and current-stage profiling

**Candidate B1: exact cost-of-context shadow audit, NO wire modification.** On fixed, independent **BWT-routed** blocks and reproducibly generated MTF token streams, compute via a source-only offline/scalar oracle:
1. Baseline actual symbol count/entropy and arithmetic order0 vs 257-context order1 *measured encoded bytes*, startup/rescale cost, symbol counts per context and context histogram reuse. Include full mode/wrapper overhead.
2. Model the **upper bound** on possible decode-time savings if contexts and block scanning were eliminated. Measure the **actual present arithmetic/context stage fraction p first**; for any target overall speedup factor `s`, Amdahl requires `p >= 1 - 1/s` even for an infinitely fast replacement. Existing A48 dickens needs ~11.08x Brotli decode parity, meaning `p >= 90.98%`, while `mr` needs ~7.05x, `p >= 85.82%`; those are severe requirements. A 15% speed improvement is not a strict-Pareto crossing.
3. If prequential predictions/context splitting save enough **complete** bytes to justify decoded bits, state, side flags and memory, preregister **one additive decoder-visible mode** with a frozen compact context selector, not per-token dynamic model updates chosen by encoder only.
4. **Falsify/PARK B1** if order-1 still loses after cold starts, context-budget savings are below added model/format overhead, profiling indicates the context stage could not close the relevant constraint, or no representative heldout has plausible improvement across the required axes. Do not build a codepath, run long CI, or claim a Hutter-style breakthrough just from entropy estimates.

**Priority:** Keep A70/libsais v2.12.2 version-only same-run decoder scout first. Then profile actual bottlenecks. B1 is a bounded, source-grounded future *decision card*, not permission to start a new hosted run. The most immediately actionable takeaway from Brotli is **cheap early decisions AND explicit decoding-cost awareness**, not copy-pasting its algorithm or more expensive q11 parsing.

## New empirical mechanism: pure Brotli passthrough cannot win the Brotli byte axis under ANV0 framing

This is an **independent raw-artifact recomputation**, not a new CI result or conjecture. The cancelled frozen A48 run `44380412243` artifact `14480494484` (ZIP SHA-256 `71b6b288b1a2d3f2ae0adf132287e0b1a790642e937ba9526018f8b678ea3eab`) contains both ANVIL `grid-out/work/{file}/anvil.cap8` and independent `brotli-q11.br` streams. An independent read-only Python parser validated source-accurate ANV0 revision-2 block lengths and sliced the actual embedded Brotli stream from each ANVIL file. **The sliced embedded stream was compared byte-for-byte with the respective reference, and they were IDENTICAL** (not merely equal length).

| File | Standalone Brotli q11/lw30 bytes | ANV0 total bytes | Outer ANV0 header | Ratio transform/backend/uvar prefix | Exact embedded Brotli stream | Complete byte disadvantage |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|---:|
| `mozilla` | 13,806,141 | 13,806,173 | 26 B | 6 B | Identical, SHA-256 `e8ced110ac79ac0ef48b1bdf2e8c085f049375ab4390d1eb60c95abb3b967a2e` | **+32 B** |
| `nci` | 1,497,411 | 1,497,442 | 25 B | 6 B | Identical, SHA-256 `5f9b438c93f671283471f364ef46730f8848b743c5f270aba5b1a1ae58cc0fff` | **+31 B** |

Both were `mode=17 / transform=0 / backend=1`, exactly one frame, with all input/output lengths and byte consumption validated. The 6-byte ratio prefix is `transform_id=0`, `backend_id=1`, and the canonical variable-length transformed-size integer. The 25–26 byte outer prefix includes ANV0 magic/revision, block-size/source-length varints, output-block length, mode, compressed payload length and CRC32; its exact length varies by file size. The internal Brotli stream is **byte-identical** to the standalone q11 reference. The observed +31/+32 B is therefore a proven ANVIL **container/envelope tax**, not a worse Brotli entropy model, different dictionary, or additional compressed inner bits.

**Engineering implication:** For a direct-Brotli passthrough that preserves the ANV0 framing and sends precisely the same Brotli bytes, `B_ANVIL = B_Brotli + header_overhead > B_Brotli`. Such a route cannot strictly dominate standalone Brotli on **complete bytes**, regardless of faster decode, caching, libsais, or arithmetic. Reducing the wrapper to some positive size alone still leaves a positive byte gap. A genuine byte-axis crossing would require a new representation/entropy gain large enough to pay the (possibly optimized) envelope, or a fundamentally different format/contract; the latter cannot silently sacrifice backend identity, checksums, streaming or backward-compatibility gates merely to remove bytes. A single-file raw Brotli passthrough cannot be mislabeled an ANV0-codec Pareto win.

**Scope limit:** This is proven for those two **actual archived** ANV0/q11 files only and the existing format; it is not a theorem that no alternative ANVIL transform can beat Brotli, nor an argument for optimizing text-only Hutter scores. Keep lossless, decoding work and competing xz/zstd axes as separate constraints. This should **PARK superficial header micro-optimizations as a route to full strict Pareto** unless a realistic representation improvement or clearly beneficial tiny-file workload makes their end-to-end cost decision-changing.

### Conditional structural tradeoff for current direct-mode auto routing

The frozen `src/anvil.cpp:4802–4836` `encode_ratio_block` implementation serially calculates Brotli q11 and BWT candidates for each eligible transform, then chooses the smaller complete payload. Consequently, for a **direct Brotli-selected** file, the candidate is the *same* Brotli stream plus nonzero ANV0 framing, while the encoder also paid the positive work of evaluating the losing BWT arm. Under the same process and work assumptions:

```
B_auto_direct_brotli = B_standalone_brotli + H,        H > 0
T_auto_arm = T_brotli_encode + T_bwt_encode + overhead
```

This provides a conditional **two-axis structural obstruction** to strict Pareto dominance over the *same standalone Brotli setting* on Brotli-selected files. The first identity is empirically byte-exact on A48 `mozilla` and `nci`. The time expression is a source-level serial-work decomposition, **not** a cross-run timing CI or a claim that any measured wall time equals the sum of independently measured programs (different library/CPU/OS noise may alter that comparison). For BWT-selected `dickens`/`mr`, ANVIL earns a real byte win but still pays q11+BWT encode and historical BWT decode was ~11.08×/~7.05× slower than Brotli. Source-level cost gating, reuse, an independently superior single algorithm, or a truly certified early-exit mechanism is required to move that operating point, rather than just micro-optimizing the decoder.

This is **not** an impossibility theorem for all future ANVIL modes; it is explicitly scoped to current serial dual evaluation, unchanged ANV0 wrapper, direct Brotli/BWT branches and same source/reference settings. It further argues against promoting Hutter-style extra prediction work without paired encode/decode costs.
