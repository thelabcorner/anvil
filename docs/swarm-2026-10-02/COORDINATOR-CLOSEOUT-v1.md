## 1. Binding research doctrine

1. The objective is **absolute Pareto-frontier movement**, not novelty-by-difference.
2. Novelty must be **mechanism-level, non-obvious, and semantically distinct** from prior art. A new placement, framing, syntax, local/global variant, or composition of occupied mechanisms is engineering unless a new step is demonstrated.
3. **Breakthrough value and novelty are separate axes.** Adopt-class engineering is fundable when it can materially improve the frontier.
4. Search gaps are **gaps, never clearance**.
5. Measured, derived, projected, historical/stale, and hypothetical values must remain distinct.
6. No post-hoc gate movement. A preregistered negative remains negative.
7. All bytes, framing, tables, dictionaries, models, selector state, code size, RSS, encode cost, decode cost, initialization, and side information are charged.
8. Heavy corpus/performance work is **GitHub-Actions-only**. Local work is limited to static analysis, bounded toy/proof fixtures, and non-heavy correctness scaffolding.
9. Same-job paired timing is required for performance claims; hosted-runner timing below the measured sensitivity floor is not decision-grade.
10. A byte-only win is not a `FRONT-CROSSING`.
11. Existing/adopt-class jobs may not emit novelty, mechanism-contribution, or frontier language without a separate novelty preregistration.
12. An upstream kill cancels downstream work; dead jobs are not run "for completeness."

## 4. Hard technical closures

### 4.1 Parser frontier lane
The canonical `--parse=ratio` route does not invoke ANVIL's candidate-generation parser. Parser/candidate-generation work cannot move that frontier without becoming a new backend/representation claim. Track 17 is closed as frontier work.

### 4.2 PRA-1 H0 safe-prune
Empirical zero-order entropy is **not** a universal lower bound on Brotli/LZ output. Repeated/correlated strings can compress below their marginal-order-0 ideal length. Therefore the PRA-1 H0 prune is not admissible and exactness is not established. No remote dispatch unless a genuinely safe bound replaces it.

### 4.3 BWT LF-walk scheduler
Post-aux measured/arithmetic stage shares show the LF walk is not large enough to meet the required whole-codec acceleration even with an idealized walk. A second lane scheduler is also a re-burn after aux-unBWT. **No WSI/lane-scheduler prototype.**

### 4.4 Token entropy / opcode-pull optimization
Measured decode-floor anatomy places opcode entropy pulls around 0.3% of end-to-end decode on the profiled path. Entropy-pull micro-optimization cannot produce a frontier-scale speedup. Do not fund on performance grounds.

### 4.5 Correction-topology reopening
Historical topology coding is already negative and masks are highly unique. A mask-ceiling diagnostic may quantify information content, but **must emit `authorizes_prototype: false`** and may not reopen a topology mechanism.
