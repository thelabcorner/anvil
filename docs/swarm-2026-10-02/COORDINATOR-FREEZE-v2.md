## 1. Binding doctrine

1. Absolute Pareto-frontier movement first; novelty-by-difference is not a research objective.
2. Mechanism novelty and engineering value are separate. Adopt-class work may be funded only for credible Pareto/enabling value.
3. Search gaps are gaps, never novelty clearance.
4. Every byte, table, model, dictionary, framing field, selector, RSS/code-size cost, initialization cost, encode cost, and decode cost is charged.
5. Measured / derived / projected / historical / hypothetical values remain explicitly separated.
6. No threshold movement after observing results.
7. Synthetic/consumed/discovery data may buy sensitivity or anatomy, never generalization.
8. A byte-only win is not a `FRONT-CROSSING`.
9. Heavy corpus/performance measurement is GitHub-Actions-only.
10. Same-job paired timing is required for performance claims; timing below the established sensitivity floor is non-decisional.
11. An upstream kill cancels downstream work. Dead jobs are not run for completeness.
12. Jobs tagged engineering/adopt-class/diagnostic may not emit novelty or frontier-contribution claims.
